## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Setup Walkthrough

**Phase 1 — Preparation**
1. Confirm your Windows machine meets the baseline above.
2. On your Android device, open Settings → About Phone → tap Build Number repeatedly to unlock Developer Options.
3. Inside Developer Options, enable USB Debugging.
4. Connect the phone to your PC with a quality USB cable.

**Phase 2 — Launch & Detect**
1. Start the FoneLab Android Rescue Vault application.
2. Wait while it detects the connected device. Approve the debugging prompt on your phone screen if it appears.
3. Select the file categories you want the scanner to seek out.

**Phase 3 — Scan**
1. Choose between a quick scan and a deep scan. Deep scans take longer but dig further into storage remnants.
2. Let the progress indicator run to completion. Avoid disconnecting the device mid-scan.

**Phase 4 — Preview & Restore**
1. Browse the recovered items by category.
2. Tick the boxes next to files you want back.
3. Choose an output folder on your PC — never overwrite the device’s own storage during recovery.
4. Confirm and export.

**Phase 5 — Verify**
1. Open the export folder and confirm the files are readable.
2. Optionally keep a copy on an external drive for redundancy.

## 🧪 Recovery Scenarios in Detail

**Scenario A — The Accidental Delete**
You cleared a folder of vacation photos while tidying up. The tool scans the device’s photo indexes and presents thumbnails for you to cherry-pick.

**Scenario B — The Factory Reset**
A reset wipes the visible layer but often leaves recoverable traces. A deep scan has the best chance here, though results depend on how much new data has been written since.

**Scenario C — The Corrupted Card**
MicroSD cards occasionally throw tantrums. The scanner attempts to read past the corruption and pull what it can.

**Scenario D — The Broken Screen**
If the phone still powers on and debugging was pre-enabled, you may recover content without ever needing to see the display.

**Scenario E — The WhatsApp Thread**
Precious conversations can be pulled from app storage remnants, subject to the app’s own retention behavior.
